Overview

Why navigate multi-step promotional gateways manually?

Educational partners periodically offer 12 months of complimentary Google AI Plus subscriptions. However, claiming them manually requires traversing multi-step profile forms, formatting regional mobile numbers, handling email confirmation challenges, and unwrapping nested redirect tokens.

The Manual Method
  • × Tedious manual entry of educational records and regional phone numbers.
  • × Switching browser tabs repeatedly to check incoming verification emails.
  • × Session timeouts and fragile authentication redirects that expire midway.
  • × Having to manually bookmark or copy down long redemption query strings.
Average manual time: 8–15 minutes of repetitive clicking
The Google AI Plus Portal Way
  • ✓ 1-click randomized demographic profiles or custom inputs with automatic validation.
  • ✓ Runs headless in the background via Playwright, attaching to your pre-installed browser.
  • ✓ Human-in-the-loop: Prompts cleanly only when your 6-digit OTP email arrives.
  • ✓ Resolves canonical https://one.google.com/offer/... link and archives to local disk.
Average runtime: ~45 seconds Zero Telemetry

Engineering Highlights

Built for reliability, privacy, and zero overhead

Engineered as a clean Windows utility using CustomTkinter and Playwright. Every feature is designed with practical desktop integration in mind.

Zero-Bloat Portable Engine

No Python runtime or manual package installs required. Automatically detects and attaches to your system's existing Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge installation, avoiding 400MB browser binary downloads.

100% Local & Private

No cloud relays, proxies, or remote APIs. Generated credentials, email addresses, and output URLs are written strictly to the local results/ folder on your drive and never transmitted externally.

Canonical Link Extraction

Unwraps session tokens and authentication redirection wrappers to deliver a clean, permanent https://one.google.com/offer/<CODE> URL that can be redeemed anytime in your browser.

Dual Delivery Architecture

Handles direct on-screen promotional offers as well as accounts where the partner dispatches activation emails directly to the inbox, providing clear status guidance for both paths.

Smart Error & Challenge Safety

Proactively detects Cloudflare Turnstile, reCAPTCHA, and gateway stops. Safely pauses execution with clear on-screen diagnostics to protect accounts from lockouts.

Built-in Diagnostics (F12)

Includes an integrated testing suite (F12) allowing developers to simulate OTP input flows, gateway rejections, and link extraction states without consuming Gmail accounts.

Google One Expectations & Limits

$0.00 Billed Today • Payment Method Required

Activating any promotional Google One subscription requires linking a valid payment method (credit/debit card, PayPal) to confirm identity and enable renewals. You are charged $0.00 today, and auto-renewal can be managed or canceled anytime in Google One account settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions

Is my Google account and password secure?

Yes, 100%. The application operates completely locally on your computer. It makes no outbound connections to external relays, servers, or analytics services. All credentials and resulting links are stored solely in the local results/ folder on your machine.

Why does the tool prompt for a 6-digit OTP code?

During promotional onboarding, the educational partner sends a 6-digit email confirmation code to your target Gmail to verify mailbox ownership. The app halts cleanly and opens an OTP verification dialog with digit filtering and paste sanitization. Once you submit the code, automation resumes immediately.

Why does Google require a credit card or PayPal during activation?

This is a standard Google One subscription requirement. Google uses a linked payment method for identity verification and continuous renewal setup. You are billed $0.00 today for the entire 12-month promotional period. You can easily cancel auto-renewal in your Google account settings at any point before the 12 months conclude.

What if a Cloudflare or reCAPTCHA verification challenge appears?

The engine proactively monitors for interstitial challenges. If one occurs, automation safely pauses to prevent lockouts, allowing you to resolve the prompt in the browser before continuing.

Do I need to install Python, Node, or Playwright browser binaries?

No. When using the standalone portable executable (Google AI Plus Portal v1.0.0.exe), all dependencies are compiled into the binary. It attaches directly to your pre-installed Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge browser.
